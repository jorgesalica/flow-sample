# Quick Overview: The Fractal Strategy in a Nutshell

> **Goal**: To stop gambling and start navigating. The market has a shape, and we can read it.

## 1. The Compass: Are we swimming or drowning?

Imagine the market has "Memory".

- **The Hurst Exponent ($H$)** is our compass.
  - **Trend ($H > 0.6$)**: The market remembers where it was going and keeps going. **Swim with the current.**
  - **Chaos ($H < 0.4$)**: The market is drunk and stumbling back and forth. **Don't swim, just float (Mean Reversion).**
  - **Random ($H \approx 0.5$)**: Total noise. **Get out of the water.**

## 2. The Map: Where is the treasure?

Price doesn't move randomly; it moves seeking **Liquidity**.

- **Fractal Nodes**: Look at previous peaks and valleys (5-bar patterns).
- **The secret**: Those points are not just "lines on a chart"; they are **Vaults of Money** (Stop Losses). The price _wants_ to go there to unlock them.

## 3. The Playbook: How to win?

We combine the Compass and the Map.

1. **Check the Compass**: Is it a Trending day? (H > 0.6)
2. **Find the Vault**: Where is the nearest Fractal Node?
3. **Wait for the Key**: Do not buy blindly. Wait for a "Micro-Fractal" to form right at the Vault door. That confirms the door is opening.

## Summary in one sentence

> We use **Math (Hurst)** to know _if_ we should trade, and **Geometry (Fractals)** to know _where_ to trade.
