# Quick Overview: Standard Trading Logic

> **Goal**: To impose order on chaos. We filter market noise through three lenses: Structure, Mathematics, and Survival.

## 1. The Context: Market Structure

Before looking at any indicator, we look at the "Terrain".

- **Support & Resistance**: These are not lines; they are **memory**. Zones where "trapped" mechanics create buying or selling pressure.
- **Breakout vs. Fakeout**:
  - **Breakout**: Price leaves a zone with **Volume** and **Conviction**.
  - **Fakeout (Trap)**: Price pokes out to steal liquidity (Stop Losses) and reverses.

## 2. The Trigger: Technical Indicators

We use math to confirm what our eyes see.

- **RSI (The Rubber Band)**: Measures speed.
  - _Divergence_: If Price goes higher but RSI goes lower, the trend is lying. Prepare for reversal.
- **MACD (The Engine Check)**: Measures momentum.
  - _Histogram_: Used to spot early turns before the crowd sees them.
- **Moving Averages (The Stream)**:
  - _Golden Cross_: Major sentiment shift (50 > 200). Use as a "Green Light" filter.

## 3. The Shield: Risk Management

This is the only thing we actually control.

- **The 1% Rule**: Never risk more than 1% of your bank on one idea.
- **Position Sizing**: If your Stop Loss is wide (far away), you buy **less**. If it's tight, you buy **more**. The loss is always the same ($).
- **Stop Loss**: Never random. Place it behind a "Wall" (Support level) or using Volatility (ATR).

## Summary in one sentence

> We find the **Location** (Structure), confirm the **Momentum** (Indicators), and then bet a **Calculated Amount** (Risk) that we can afford to lose.
