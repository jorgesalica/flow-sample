# Quick Overview: The Unified Strategy

> **Goal**: To navigate the chaos using a Fractal Compass (The "Where" & "If") and Standard Tactics (The "When" & "How").

## 1. The Assessment (Hurst Regime)

First, we look at the weather.

- **Persistent ($H > 0.6$)**: The market is flowing. **Strategy: Trend**. Look for Breakouts and Pullbacks (Hidden Divergence).
- **Mean Reverting ($H < 0.4$)**: The market is chopping. **Strategy: Range**. Look for Fakes (Liquidity Sweeps) and Reversals (Regular Divergence).
- **Random ($H \approx 0.5$)**: The market is noise. **Strategy: Cash**. Do nothing.

## 2. The Location (Structure & Nodes)

We only act at Key Locations.

- **Fractal Nodes**: Previous turning points. These are the "Vaults" of liquidity.
- **The Check**:
  - **Breakout**: Price blasts through the Vault and stays there + Volume.
  - **Fakeout**: Price pokes the Vault to steal money (Stop Run) and falls back.

## 3. The Confirmation (Technical Signals)

We narrow the timing.

- **In Trends**: Use **MACD** and **Moving Averages** to enter on strength.
- **In Ranges**: Use **RSI Divergence** to enter on exhaustion (reversals).

## 4. The Shield (Risk Protocol)

We survive to play another round.

- **Size**: $Risk / (Entry - Stop)$. Usually 1% of total equity.
- **Stop**: Placed logically behind Structure or taking Volatility (ATR) into account.
- **Rule**: If the potential win is less than 2x the risk, **ABORT**.

## Summary in one sentence

> We check the **Regime** (Hurst) to pick the right **Strategy**, wait for price to hit a **Fractal Node**, confirm with **Indicators**, and execute only if the **Math (Risk/Reward)** makes sense.
